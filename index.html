Our Story 💜

Three years of craving… and then forever started.

🌸

Remember how it all started…

We didn’t meet in a cafe. We didn’t bump into each other on a street. We met online — and somehow, that felt more real than anything. On 16th September 2023, I proposed to you… and I was scared. Properly scared. But you said yes. And from that moment, my world quietly changed. That “yes” still lives in my heart.

Our first little universe ✨

🦋

Those three years… we only had a screen

For three long years, we lived on video calls — wishing, waiting, craving. Every “good night” felt incomplete. Every “I miss you” felt louder. And then it happened — because of you, Trisha. You made “someday” turn into “today”. That wait was hard… but you were always worth it.

Outside the screen… finally us 🌿

Moments that feel like home 🥰

💻

And then we finally met…

Our first time together, you chose a movie. We were lying on the bed, laptop open… But honestly? My concentration was not on that movie at all. I kept slowly, slowly moving closer — trying to hug you — and you got all shy. That little shy moment? Still makes me smile like an idiot.

Another day that felt like magic 🌸

Soft memories, loud heartbeats 💕

🎬

That day in the theatre…

I say every day with you is special — and it’s true. But that day in the theatre? That one is locked forever. While the movie played… we started kissing. Time paused. The world blurred. And I knew — this memory is mine for life.

💫

You became more than “girlfriend”…

You’re smart. You’re intelligent. You’re sensitive — my little crying baby with a big heart and a thousand emotions. And you love me… so loudly, so softly, so completely. I love when you act cute without even knowing you’re cute. When you talk. When you do silly things. Whatever you do — you look cute. Every. Single. Time. And ordinary days? You make them feel expensive. When you order food for me online — I feel so cared for. You know my mood. You know my taste. You know me. And that funny thing I love forever: when we eat together and I finish first… you quietly give me food from your side. It’s small. It’s funny. It’s the sweetest thing in the world. Also — our dhaba nights? Kaju paneer forever. Then waffles, faluda, ice cream… teasing, sharing, laughing. That’s us. That’s happiness.

✨

And today… it’s our day

Today is not just a date on the calendar. It’s the day I found the courage to ask… and you gave me the softest, bravest “yes”. Happy Anniversary, Trisha. Thank you for choosing me — online, offline, and every day after.
